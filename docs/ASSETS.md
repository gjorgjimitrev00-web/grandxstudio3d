# Development imagery

The three original raster images in `public/images/` were generated for this project with imagegen. They depict conceptual 3D printed products: a ribbed lamp still life, a controller cradle, and a blue headphone stand. WebP derivatives are the delivered assets.

These are development design concepts, not photographs of the business's verified products. Sample records reuse these images to demonstrate catalog, variants and checkout. Before publishing actual inventory, upload accurate photographs of each product and replace all sample descriptions and prices.

No claims about availability, manufacturing certification, electrical compliance, environmental benefits, or compatibility are implied by these concept illustrations.
